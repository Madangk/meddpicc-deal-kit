# MEDDPICC question bank

Pick 3 to 5 questions per call for the elements you know least about. Listen for the red flags.

## Metrics
- What does success look like 6 months after go-live, in numbers?
- How do you measure this today?
- What is this problem costing you per month, in time, money or risk?
- Who would care most if that number improved?

**Red flags:** "We just want to explore." No baseline. Nobody owns the number.

## Economic buyer
- Who signs off on a project of this size?
- Has this person agreed this is a priority this year?
- What does that person care about most right now?
- Can we get 20 minutes with them to confirm we're solving the right problem?

**Red flags:** The champion avoids introducing you. "They'll approve whatever I recommend."

## Decision criteria
- What will you use to compare options?
- Which of those matter most, and why?
- What would a POC or trial have to prove?
- Are there technical, security or compliance must-haves?

**Red flags:** Criteria that read like a competitor's datasheet.

## Decision process
- Walk me through how you'll make this decision, from now to signature.
- Who else is involved at each step?
- How did you buy the last tool like this?
- What could slow it down?

**Red flags:** "We'll figure it out." Steps with no dates.

## Paper process
- Once you decide, what happens: legal, security review, procurement?
- How long did the last vendor take to get through procurement?
- Do you buy through a cloud marketplace or directly?
- Is budget approved, or does it need a new request?

**Red flags:** Unknown security review. Fiscal year ending soon with no budget.

## Identify pain
- What made you look at this now?
- What happens if you do nothing for another 6 months?
- Who feels this problem most?
- What have you already tried?

**Red flags:** Pain only our side can see. No trigger event.

## Champion
- Why does this matter to you personally?
- Who would push back on this, and how would you handle it?
- Can you help me get time with the economic buyer?
- Would you share what the others are saying internally?

**Champion test:** Ask them to do something for the deal (an intro, a document, a meeting). A real champion follows through.

## Competition
- What else are you considering, including building it yourself or doing nothing?
- What do you like about the other options?
- Who are you using today, and what would make you switch?

**Red flags:** "No one else." Late entry into an RFP we didn't help shape.
