# MEDDPICC scorecard

Use [`meddpicc-scorecard.csv`](meddpicc-scorecard.csv) as a spreadsheet: download it and open it in Excel or Google Sheets. One row per element. Update it after every call.

## Scoring guide

| Element | 0: Unknown | 1: Assumed | 2: Confirmed by one contact | 3: Confirmed and agreed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Metrics** | No numbers | We think it saves X | Champion agrees on a metric | Buyer has a written business case with numbers |
| **Economic buyer** | Don't know who | Guess by title | Named, not met | Met, and agrees the problem is a priority |
| **Decision criteria** | Unknown | Assumed from research | Shared by champion | Written criteria we helped shape |
| **Decision process** | Unknown | Assumed steps | Steps described by champion | Steps, people and dates confirmed |
| **Paper process** | Unknown | Assumed | Legal, security and procurement steps known | Steps and timeline agreed and in the MAP |
| **Identify pain** | No pain found | Our hypothesis | Buyer states the pain | Pain has a cost and an owner |
| **Champion** | None | Friendly contact | Contact with influence who shares info | Tested champion who sells for us internally |
| **Competition** | Unknown | Assumed | Known alternatives, including doing nothing | We know how we win against each |

## Health score

Add up the eight scores (max 24).

| Total | Status | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 9 | Early or at risk | Don't forecast. Focus on pain, champion and economic buyer. |
| 10 to 16 | Developing | Close the lowest-scoring gaps before committing a date. |
| 17 to 24 | Strong | Forecast, and lock the paper process in the mutual action plan. |

A deal with **0 on Economic buyer or Champion** stays out of commit, whatever the total.
